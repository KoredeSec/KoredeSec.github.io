About

The background, skills, and certifications behind the security work on this site.

I'm a security engineer with hands-on experience across the full security lifecycle: penetration testing, detection engineering, cloud infrastructure hardening, and governance. I approach security not as a checklist but as a practice: understanding how systems break, and building the controls and visibility to keep them from breaking at scale.

I've completed two structured training programs (the Cyblack SOC Academy and the AWS re/Start program) and a six-sprint security internship covering threat intelligence, cloud security, GRC, and SOC operations. My academic foundation is a BSc in Cybersecurity from Osun State University (UNIOSUN), where I also served as the Pioneer President of NACSS UNIOSUN across two administrations, from 2024 to 2026.
