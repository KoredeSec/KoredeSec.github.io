Leadership

Pioneer President of the Nigeria Association of Cyber Security Students (NACSS), Osun State University, across two administrations.

I served as the Pioneer President of the Nigeria Association of Cyber Security Students (NACSS), Osun State University, across two administrations from 2024 to 2026. My time in office was focused on building a strong cybersecurity community and creating opportunities for students to learn, compete, network and gain practical exposure beyond the classroom.

Across both administrations, I led and coordinated major initiatives including NACSS Cyber Fairs, cybersecurity bootcamps, ISC training programs, hackathons and NACSS Week, bringing together students around technical learning, professional development and community. I also worked closely with my executive team to create opportunities for students to engage with cybersecurity professionals and gain a better understanding of the industry and the different career paths available within it.

A major part of my presidency was turning ideas into actual programs. I was involved in planning and coordinating events, developing the materials used to promote them, working with speakers and participants, and driving student participation. I wanted NACSS to be more than a departmental association on paper; I wanted it to be a platform where students could actively build skills, discover opportunities and connect with the cybersecurity ecosystem.

Beyond NACSS activities, I represented the Department of Cybersecurity within the wider Faculty of Computing and Information Technology (FOCITSA), contributing to the broader student leadership community. My leadership and service during my university years were recognised with four awards: Departmental President of the Year (FOCITSA), Cloud Security Analyst of the Year, Security Analyst of the Year, and Award of Service.

Serving across two administrations gave me the opportunity to not only help establish NACSS, but also build on that foundation and grow it into a more active platform for cybersecurity education, practical learning, professional exposure and student development.
